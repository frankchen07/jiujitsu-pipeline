jiujitsu-pipeline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5th, 2026

jiujitsu-pipeline is a set of macOS shell scripts (process.sh and friends) that encode videos on your computer and upload them to your own YouTube channel. It runs entirely on your machine — there is no server, account, or backend. This policy explains what data is (and isn't) collected.

What it accesses

  • Your video files — the scripts read videos from local folders you configure and write encoded copies back to local folders.
  • Video metadata — creation time and embedded GPS location are read locally to pick a gym location code and build the filename. The script compares coordinates against locations you configure; it never sends them anywhere itself.
  • Your YouTube account — through Google OAuth, to upload videos and add them to playlists (see below).

Google and YouTube data

Uploads use the YouTube Data API via the open-source youtubeuploader CLI, which requests these OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to your channel
  • youtube — to set video privacy and add videos to your playlists
  • youtubepartner — requested by the uploader tool by default; jiujitsu-pipeline does not use any partner features

The OAuth token is saved only on your computer, in a file named request.token. It is used only to upload your own videos to your own channel, set each video's title and privacy (unlisted or public), and add it to your own playlists. The only data kept from YouTube is the ID of each uploaded video, which is written to a local log.

jiujitsu-pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What gets sent to YouTube

Only the video file you are uploading, its title, its privacy setting, and the playlist it belongs to. Before upload, all embedded metadata — including GPS location — is stripped from the YouTube copy. Location data stays only in your local archive copy. YouTube's handling of uploaded content is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No data sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer
  • No reading of your YouTube comments, subscribers, watch history, or other channel data
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data to anyone

Local storage

Logs are written to a local logs/ folder: run logs, a ledger of original → renamed filenames, and a list of uploaded YouTube video IDs. They never leave your computer.

Third parties

The only external service is Google's YouTube Data API, used solely for the uploads described above. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You can revoke access at any time by deleting request.token and removing the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the project folder removes all local logs and videos.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub. See also the Terms of Service.
